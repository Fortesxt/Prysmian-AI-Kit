The COTF Prompt Framework

A universal model for writing prompts that actually work — for any AI tool

C
Context
Tell the AI who you are, what your role is, and the situation you're in.
O
Objective
State clearly what you want the AI to do. Be specific and direct.
T
Tone
Define the voice: formal, friendly, technical, executive-level, etc.
F
Format
Specify the output: bullet list, email, table, summary in 3 sentences…
Example prompt using COTF
[Context] I'm a sales analyst preparing for a quarterly business review. [Objective] Summarize the main risks and opportunities from this data. [Tone] Executive-level, concise, no jargon. [Format] 3 bullet points for risks, 3 for opportunities.
